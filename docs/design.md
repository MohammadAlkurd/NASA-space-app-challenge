# Design brief

**One line:** a Mars mission-planning map you can step into. **Player:** students, judges, the public; no instructions needed.
**Promise:** click a place on real Mars terrain, get a safe way there, and understand why it is safe.

## Core loop
1. Look: pick a target on the map or a science stop.
2. Plan: compare 2-4 routes (safest, fastest, low radiation, science-rich) by time, risk, dose and suit time or battery.
3. Go: follow the route in 3D with helmet guidance; weather, dose and reserves change as time passes.
4. Learn: arrive at a science stop, see Perseverance's photo and why the place matters. Repeat, or draw your own route and compare.

Fail states are soft: no-go slopes block movement and are explained; low suit time, battery or sunset raise warnings.

## Level plan (Jezero, 5.12 km square)
- Start: Octavia E. Butler Landing, rover parked, astronaut beside it. Flat, safe ground.
- Near targets (under 100 m to 1 km): Van Zyl Overlook, Roubion, Rochette: easy first routes.
- Mid: South Seitah (Brac, Issole): sand ripples, Be careful ratings.
- Far (2.5-3 km): Three Forks depot, Amalik, Skinner/Wildcat Ridge on the delta front: slopes where the astronaut and rover routes differ.
- Camera: third person behind the traveller, helmet view, top-down map.

## Scope cuts (in order, if time runs out)
Globe intro, second site, rocks and dust polish, mobile touch layout.
